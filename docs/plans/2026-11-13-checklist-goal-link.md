# Checklist x Goal: every university, every Portfolio / Quota project

## Goal
Checklist covers TCAS round 1 (Portfolio) and round 2 (Quota) for every programme MyTCAS lists, and is
connected to the Goal app: the student's goal programmes show up in Checklist with their own round 1/2
projects, each with a checklist the student can tick.

## Data (no hand-written list can cover this, so two layers)
1. **Project snapshot (generated).** `scripts/buildChecklistProjects.mjs` reads MyTCAS (course list +
   `program-projects` per programme) and writes `public/data/checklist/projects/<universityId>.json`
   (+ `manifest.json`). Per project: round, id, name, seats, accepted applicant types, min GPAX, min total
   score, required exam scores, conditions text, interview info, official announcement link.
   Sharded per university and fetched on demand, so the app stays fast.
2. **Hand-verified lists (existing).** `src/data/checklists/*.json`: exam content (TGAT / A-Level) and
   university process lists read from official announcements. These are shown as "ขั้นตอนของมหาวิทยาลัย".

## Checklist of one project (generated by `projectChecklist(project, round)`)
- Eligibility checks from the data (applicant type, GPAX, total score, exam scores, seats).
- Documents: round template (Portfolio / Quota) + items taken from the project's conditions.
- Dates and interview from the data, link to the official announcement (always shown).
- Ticks are stored per `programId:projectId` on this device.

## Goal link
- Checklist opens on "เป้าหมายของฉัน": for each goal (useLifeStore.goals, `programId`) lists its round 1 / 2
  projects (loaded from the university shard), with progress.
- Goal app: each goal card gets a shortcut into its Checklist.
- Programme without a `programId` (manual goal): falls back to university process lists.
